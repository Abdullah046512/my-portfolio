About me

Learning by building,
not just reading.

Hello, I am Abdullah Bashir, an aspiring cybersecurity analyst. I am still a student, and I am honest about that — everything on this page is something I am actively learning or something I have built myself in my own lab.

My way of learning is simple: I study one idea, then I write a small program to test it. That is how I got into networking, Linux and Python. Right now I am focusing on three areas: cybersecurity, networking and Linux.

All of my practice happens on my own computer. I only ever test systems I own or have explicit written permission to test.
